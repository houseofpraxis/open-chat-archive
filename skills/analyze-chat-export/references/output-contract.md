# Output contract

Model-visible: policy version, opaque alias paths, types, bounded buckets, structural coordinates, synthetic fixtures, generated source/tests, and optional locally approved semantic labels.

Restricted/model-forbidden: source bytes and paths, raw keys/values and their hashes, protected alias map, real conformed batches, local execution traces, secrets, and canaries.

Diagnostics contain fixed codes and approved coordinates. Scaffold ownership is enumerated in `OWNED_FILES.txt`. The adapter protocol provides `recognize` and `conform`, returning typed fingerprint/batch results and safe diagnostics.
